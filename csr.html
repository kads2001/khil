Kamat Hotels (India) Ltd.
Seed Ball Initiative

Our sustainable hotels in India are focusing on helping reduce the costs of planting various useful indigenous plant species (mostly trees!). The ball helps protect the seed within from predators such as birds, rodents and insects and extremes of temperature until the rains arrive!

The seed-balls that are easier to plant as compared to saplings are made from mud, organic compost and seeds. The advantage of the seed-balls is that they can be thrown to any land to grow, provided that the soil is not too dry or dense with other vegetation.

The Orchid celebrated World Environment Week and distributed around 2500 pouches of vermicompost and basil seed packs and spread the message of the need to combat global warming and the importance of planting trees and managing solid waste. Also, interesting documentaries on wildlife were screened, and several fun eco games were organized for the kids.

What are seed balls?

A Seed ball is simply that - a seed inside of a ball of mud and compost, mixed with some nutritious binders. Once soaked, the seed ball will help retain and prolong a moist environment around the seed to encourage germination.

How to make?

Just store the seed ball in a dry place and throw it anywhere you want the tree to grow. Yes! It's that simple. Nature will take care of the rest.

Kamat Hotels (India) Ltd.
Ganpatree Initiative

Every year, millions of Ganesh idols, small, large and humongous, are drowned in rivers, lakes, and seas around the world during the Ganesh Chaturthi festival. As part of the ‘Visarjan’, these Ganesh idols make their way to the nearest water bodies where they are then casually submerged into the water. But they don’t dissolve immediately due to the materials used while preparing these Idols. With time, the paint and other synthetic materials on the idols peel off and start floating around in the water causing pollution. Airborne contaminants from these chemicals spread far and wide, polluting everything in close vicinity.

In order to avoid this, the concept of Ganpatree had been introduced, wherein the idol is made up of red soil, organic fertilizer and vegetable seeds. The seeds are mixed with mud to make the idol and they are also inserted into the mud pot. Organic fertilizer does not allow the seeds to dry up. On the submersion day, we don't immerse, but water It. Instead of immersing the idol into the waterbody, people can start watering the idol everyday. Due to this, the idol will start melting in the mud pot and the seed germination process will begin immediately. Ganpatree is one of the ecological approaches that The Orchid Hotel advocates and follows.

Corporate Social Responsibility

KHIL Initiatives

The major initiatives taken by the Company under Corporate Social Responsibility are in the area of the environment. ‘The Orchid’, flagship hotel of the Company, has taken various initiatives for creating awareness and conservation of the environment. Among these, The Orchid has adopted and maintains gardens and traffic islands around Mumbai city for beautification purposes and improvement of the overall environment.

The Orchid, along with the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, has initiated the Nirmalya Project wherein Nirmalya generated during the Ganesh festival is collected, segregated and converted into valuable vermicompost to ensure that it is not dumped. Nirmalya Project was acknowledged by granting The Golden Star- Most Admired CSR practice of the Year Award by the Stars of the Industry group.

The Orchid celebrated World Environment Week and distributed around 2,500 pouches of vermicompost and basil seed packs to spread the message of the need to combat global warming and the importance of planting trees and managing solid waste, alongside wildlife documentary screenings and fun eco games for kids. Additionally, old plastic cans converted into nest boxes have been set up in the hotel lane to conserve sparrows and distributed to hotel employees and guests.

The Orchid is one of the few hotels to spread environmental awareness to students of schools and colleges who are invited regularly, where they are educated about solid waste management, energy efficiency, water conservation and preservation, vermiculture, and a 4-bin segregation system. During the year under review, more than 300 students visited the hotel. Several events including poster competitions, wealth out of waste contests, quizzes, photography competitions, and interactive sessions with NGO Greenpeace were also held to increase awareness of environmental issues.

Earth Hour was observed in The Orchid by switching off non-essential lights to accomplish the endeavour to mobilize action against climate change. A silent rally was conducted by Green team members before Diwali to urge the public to reduce air and noise pollution by not using firecrackers. Furthermore, as a caring corporate citizen, our Company has recruited a reasonable number of physically challenged persons, thereby providing meaningful employment opportunities to this underprivileged segment of society.

Philanthropic Initiative

The Indira Venkatesh Foundation

Founded in 1995 by Dr. Vithal Venkatesh Kamat, the Indira Venkatesh Foundation stands as a testament to the belief that women should not only be recipients of favors but self-proclaimed givers, empowering others and contributing positively to society. The foundation takes its name from Dr. Kamat's beloved mother, Indira Venkatesh, whose legacy of compassion and giving continues to inspire its mission.

One of the remarkable initiatives spearheaded by Dr. Kamat is the creation of the AAI (Mother) Museums in Mumbai and Pune. These museums pay homage to mothers worldwide, celebrating the vital role they have played throughout history in giving birth to, nurturing, and shaping great individuals. Through engaging exhibits, the AAI Museums shed light on the evolution of culture and customs, while also highlighting the profound influence of women in molding the destinies of great men.

At the core of the foundation's objectives is the aim to uplift the underprivileged and self-sufficient girls, transforming their lives through education. Dr. Kamat's vision goes beyond mere academics, as he believes in nurturing the entrepreneurial spirit within these young minds. By providing education and vocational training, the foundation equips girls with the tools and knowledge they need to become successful entrepreneurs, fostering self-reliance and empowerment.

Girl Child Education

Empowering Women and Fostering Change

One of the pivotal areas of focus for the Indira Venkatesh Foundation is collaborating with various well-known NGOs in India to raise literacy rates among girls. These carefully chosen organizations not only sponsor the education of these girls but also provide regular updates on their progress. This approach ensures that every rupee spent goes directly towards building a contented and educated populace, enabling the creation of a stronger and more prosperous nation.

Through its unwavering commitment to education, empowerment, and social work, the Indira Venkatesh Foundation continues to create a lasting impact in the lives of countless individuals. Dr. Kamat's visionary leadership and dedication to uplifting women and underprivileged communities have transformed the foundation into a beacon of hope and change.

As the foundation looks towards the future, its mission remains rooted in the belief that empowered women can catalyze positive change and drive social progress. The Indira Venkatesh Foundation serves as an inspiring reminder that when we invest in women's education and empowerment, we invest in the betterment of society as a whole. With each step forward, the foundation paves the way for a more equitable and inclusive world, where the legacy of Indira Venkatesh lives on through the lives of those it touches.
