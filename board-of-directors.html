Dr. Vithal Venkatesh Kamat

Founder, Environmentalist & Visionary Pioneer

"Sustainable hospitality is not an option; it is our sacred responsibility toward future generations and planet Earth."

Dr. Vithal Venkatesh Kamat, a second-generation restaurateur, a pioneer in green hotels, an environmentalist, an entrepreneur, an educationalist, an antiquarian, an ornithologist, an author, a globetrotter, and a black belt holder in karate, is a versatile personality. Having begun his journey in a small, family-owned vegetarian restaurant, Dr. Vithal Kamat today is Chairman and Managing Director of Kamat Hotels (India) Limited, pioneering Asia's First Ecotel Certification.

🌱 Pioneer in Green Ecotels 📚 Renowned Author & Antiquarian 🥋 Karate Black Belt 🦅 Ornithologist & Educationalist
