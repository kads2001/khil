Our Journey

Explore the Milestones

Decades of Heritage, Innovation & Sustainable Hospitality

The Beginning

Late Shri Venkatesh Kamat started Satkar Hotel in Mumbai at Churchgate.

1958

Iconic Restaurants

Opened various iconic restaurants including Samrat at Churchgate, Status at Nariman Point, Santoor and many more.

1970 1985

Incorporation of KHIL

Kamat Hotels (India) Limited (KHIL) incorporated on 21st March 1986. Opened multi-hotels under Kamat brand at Silvassa, Ankleshwar, Vapi and Goa.

1986

Asia's First Ecotel

The Orchid Mumbai opened on 27th September 1997, pioneering Asia's first certified Eco-Friendly 5-star hotel.

1997

Public Listing

KHIL went public through a successful Initial Public Offering (IPO), fueling national hospitality ambitions.

1994

Kamat Plaza & Disco

Purchased 4-star property 'Kamat Plaza Hotel' near Mumbai Airport. Upgraded and launched the iconic 'Go Bananas'.

1990

The Orchid Mumbai

Solidified landmark green hospitality status with international sustainable luxury standards.

1997

Fort JadhavGADH

Opened the first and only heritage fort hotel of Maharashtra, preserving glorious 300-year Maratha history.

2007

The Orchid Pune

Extended The Orchid luxury eco-hospitality brand into Pune with benchmark environmental architecture.

2007

Himalayan Foothills

Opened eco-friendly Ecotel Hotels in Shimla and Manali, bringing sustainable hospitality to the north.

2021

The Orchid Lonavala

Launched company's 3rd signature Ecotel hotel nestled in scenic Lonavala hills.

2020

Balewadi & Eastern Exp.

Opened The Orchid Pune Balewadi and expanded into Orissa with Bhubaneswar, Lotus Konark & Mahabodhi Palace.

2007 2008

IRA Launch & Growth

Expanded with The Orchid Hotel Jamnagar, and launched 'IRA by Orchid Hotels' in Sambhajinagar, Bhubaneswar, and Nashik.

2023

Ayodhya, Noida & Toyam

Added IRA by Orchid Hotels in Ayodhya and Noida, along with the luxury brand Toyam by Orchid.

2024

Multi-City Milestone

Opened The Orchid Hotels in Passaros, Chandigarh, Rishikesh, and Panchgani, plus IRA in Hyderabad, Dwarka, and Goa–Porvorim.

2025

Continuing The Legacy

Expanded the IRA by Orchid Hotels portfolio with the grand opening of Bhavnagar.
