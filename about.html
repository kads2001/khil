Our Founder's Legacy

Some legacies are inherited. Others are built through relentless hard work, vision, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

The foundation of Kamat Hotels (India) Limited traces back to Late Shri Venkatesh Krishna Kamat, a pioneering entrepreneur whose journey embodied the true spirit of Indian hospitality. Starting from modest beginnings in Mumbai, he built his reputation one guest at a time, believing that exceptional food, impeccable service, and genuine warmth would always stand the test of time.

In 1958, he established Satkar Restaurant opposite Churchgate Station in Mumbai. What began as a single restaurant soon became synonymous with quality, consistency, and authentic hospitality. His dedication led to the creation of several iconic dining destinations, including Samrat, Status, Santoor, and other renowned establishments that became landmarks in Mumbai's culinary landscape. Every venture reflected his philosophy that guests should always be treated with honesty, respect, and heartfelt care.

Growing up amidst this culture of service was his son, Dr. Vithal Venkatesh Kamat. Rather than inheriting success, he earned it by learning every aspect of the business from the ground up. From observing kitchen operations and restaurant service to understanding guest expectations and business management, he immersed himself in the values his father had established. This hands-on experience shaped his entrepreneurial vision while reinforcing the importance of innovation without compromising tradition.

Recognizing the immense potential of India's evolving hospitality industry, Dr. Vithal Kamat envisioned taking the family legacy beyond restaurants. In 1986, he founded Kamat Hotels (India) Limited (KHIL), marking the family's entry into the organised hotel sector. Under his leadership, the company expanded into luxury hotels, heritage properties, resorts, and hospitality management, setting new benchmarks for service excellence and sustainable hospitality.

His pioneering vision reached global recognition with the launch of The Orchid Mumbai in 1997, Asia's first certified Ecotel hotel. The Orchid demonstrated that environmental responsibility and luxury hospitality could coexist, earning international acclaim and establishing KHIL as a pioneer in sustainable hospitality.

Today, the Kamat legacy spans three generations. What began as a single restaurant founded on hard work and integrity has evolved into one of India's most respected hospitality groups. Across every hotel, resort, and restaurant, the guiding principles remain unchanged—warm hospitality, uncompromising quality, continuous innovation, and an enduring commitment to creating memorable guest experiences.

The story of KHIL is not merely the growth of a company, it is the continuation of a legacy built on trust, strengthened by vision, and carried forward with purpose for generations to come.

About Us

Who We Are

Kamat Hotels (India) Limited was incorporated on 21st March, 1995 in the State of Maharashtra by Late Mr. Venkatesh Krishna Kamat and his associates with the main object of setting up and running of hotels and related business.

The Group is headed by Dr Vithal Venkatesh Kamat who has been spearheading the Kamat Group growth. Now he is the Chief Mentor to the next generation, Mr Vishal Kamat and the host of Professional team members who work under his guidance and experience.

KHIL’s most globally recognized brand is THE ORCHID, An Ecotel Hotel, Asia’s first chain of a 5-star, environment-sensitive hotel which has won over 95 National & International awards. The Orchid is in Mumbai, Pune and many more location.
